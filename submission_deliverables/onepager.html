1. The Critical Clinical Problem

The days immediately following hospital discharge represent the highest-risk vulnerability window in the continuum of care.

  • Discharge Summary Disconnect: Summaries are dense, abbreviated medical English. Patients and family caregivers leave confused about medication changes and red flags.
  • Medication Hazards: Complex regimen changes frequently cause accidental double-dosing, missed critical doses, and severe OTC drug-drug interactions.
  • Silent Deterioration: Congestive fluid overload or postoperative infection begins days before an emergency readmission, with no remote visibility.
2. The Solution: Four Continuous Care Loops
Loop 1 · DECODE (Discharge AI Parser)
Ingests unstructured discharge notes and maps to plain language in English, Bahasa Malaysia, 中文, or தமிழ் with verbatim source-span highlighting.
Loop 2 · PROTECT (Medication Guardian)
Reconciles active prescriptions against OTC items. Detects fatal drug-drug interactions (e.g. NSAID + Blood Thinner, Triple Whammy) before ingestion.
Loop 3 · WATCH (60s Check-in & Rules Engine)
Tracks weight, vitals, and symptoms. Computes an explainable 0–100 Recovery Risk Index with transparent factor attribution (Green / Amber / Red).
Loop 4 · CONNECT (Caregiver & SBAR Clinician Brief)
Automatically alerts designated family caregivers upon clinical deviation and auto-generates a one-tap clinical SBAR handover summary for doctors.

4. Key Technical Differentiators
  • Offline-First Demo Mode: Runs entirely client-side with zero server dependency, zero API key requirement, and zero network data leakage.
  • Traceability & Trust: Every plain-language item maps to exact character spans in the clinical discharge summary.
  • Live Ground-Truth Evaluation: Evaluator computes live precision (100.0%) and recall (100.0%) — 100% F1 across 20 synthetic summaries.
  • Universal Safety Net: Always-present emergency protocol displays immediate 999/112 dialer with patient address and active drug list.

1 The Critical Clinical Problem

The 30 days following hospital discharge represent the highest-risk vulnerability window in the continuum of care:

  • Discharge Summary Disconnect: Jargon-dense medical notes leave patients and families confused regarding crucial regimen adjustments and warning signs.
  • Medication Hazards: Complex changes trigger duplicate therapy, missed critical doses, and lethal OTC drug-drug clashes.
  • Silent Deterioration: Congestive volume creep or surgical site infection develops days before an avoidable, high-cost readmission.
2 The Solution: Four Continuous Care Loops
Loop 1 · DECODE Discharge NLP Parser & Live Sandbox
Extracts unstructured summaries across 6 languages with dual reading modes (Simple ⟷ Standard ICD-10), Live Note Sandbox re-parsing, 5 quick clinical presets, and verbatim source-span traceability.
Loop 2 · PROTECT Medication Guardian
Reconciles active prescriptions against OTC products. Deterministically catches fatal interactions (e.g. NSAID + Anticoagulant, Triple Whammy AKI) before ingestion via a 30-pair clinical matrix.
Loop 3 · WATCH 60s Check-in & Rules
Monitors daily dry weights, vitals, and surgical signs. Computes an explainable 0–100 Recovery Risk Index with transparent factor attribution (Green / Amber / Red). Time Machine tracks trajectory Day 0–3.
Loop 4 · CONNECT Caregiver Pass & SBAR
Generates cryptographically signed offline QR passes (/pass/token) for secure family surveillance and produces instant standardized SBAR medical handovers for outpatient clinical reviews.
